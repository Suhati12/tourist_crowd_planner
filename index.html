Academic & Technical Overview

About Tourist Crowd Level Planner

This project demonstrates how historical tourism analytics can be transformed into actionable visit optimization algorithms for tourists, reducing wait times and improving sightseeing experiences.

Predictive Algorithm Logic

The backend calculation engine evaluates four key predictive multipliers:

  • Base Attraction Weight: Inherent popularity score of the monument.
  • Day of Week Factor: Weekend surges (Saturday +30%, Sunday +35%).
  • Seasonality Factor: High season (Oct–Mar for heritage monuments, May–Jun for hill stations).
  • Hourly Curve: Midday peak congestion vs early morning golden hours.
